# A050 tail-v3: current teacher-free result and HairGS controls

Release date: 2026-09-09. Status: training and image evaluation complete;
**this update does not demonstrate an overall improvement over equal-budget GS**.
Here, equal budget means exactly 64,717 rasterized primitives, not equal training
compute, optimization settings, parameter count, or deployment bytes. The GS
comparator is the HairGS **compressed-finetune animal control**, not a new
from-scratch fixed-budget 3DGS experiment.

## Protocol and claim boundary

- Case: `A050_horned_cow`, `animals_a050_tail_formal_v3_20260908`.
- Identical 48 training / 12 held-out views and GT; all final renders and image
  evaluation are 2048 x 2048. Ours trains at 512 (steps < 1000), 1024 (steps
  1000-2999), then 2048 (steps 3000-7999); HairGS trains at native 2048 throughout.
  Do not label ours as native-2K-only training, or insert it into the strict
  native-2K A003 comparison table.
- Ours uses train RGB, cameras and alpha only, SH degree 2, a learnable visual-hull
  envelope, zero initial Strand primitives, and no teacher/MVS/geometry-GT/test
  inputs. Its cap is 239,171; the final actual count is 64,717, not a 75% ratio claim.
- HairGS starts from 9,167 train-only hull PointGaussian seeds: 8,000 point-stage
  steps (densification through 6,000), conversion, then 5,000 HairGS steps. It uses
  SH degree 3, mask weight 0.01, orientation/smoothness weights 0, and no HairGS
  merging/growth in the segment stage. This is an RGB-only animal adapter, not a
  claim to reproduce every original HairGS training component.
- Compact HairGS aggregates the completed 616,005-GS model to 64,717, then performs
  8,000 fixed-topology fine-tuning steps. Its dense pretraining cost is not omitted.
- All image results use `scripts/evaluate_external_renders.py` unchanged, SHA256
  `3f3bddd0b40fc86140b14203a4413f1ad0217ed0727756953a64fa69a2047bb6`.
  GT foreground is binary alpha > 0.5; foreground error uses straight GT RGB;
  full-image GT is RGB times that binary mask. Predictions are native black-background
  composites, with no second multiplication by prediction alpha. Soft-alpha
  diagnostics are not mixed into this table.

## Verified image results

PSNR is in dB. Higher PSNR, SSIM and IoU are better; lower LPIPS, FN and FP are better.
FN/FP below are the evaluator's fractions, not percentages or 3D geometry errors.

| Method | Split | Actual GS | FG PSNR | Full PSNR | mSSIM | mLPIPS | IoU | FN | FP |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| HairGS unrestricted | train48 | 616,005 | 22.86293 | 29.91083 | .932614 | .077569 | .990822 | .003856 | .005379 |
| HairGS compressed + 8k fine-tune | train48 | 64,717 | 21.09399 | 28.23020 | .897476 | .136715 | .990853 | .005566 | .003617 |
| Ours teacher-free Shell/Strand | test12 | 64,717 | 20.10302 | 26.98283 | .870356 | .174822 | .989153 | .002688 | .008299 |
| HairGS unrestricted | test12 | 616,005 | 20.07420 | 27.29600 | .894569 | .090872 | .988314 | .004247 | .007559 |
| HairGS compressed + 8k fine-tune | test12 | 64,717 | 20.02215 | 27.29259 | .886246 | .140080 | .989418 | .005941 | .004699 |

At equal actual count, ours minus compact HairGS on test12 is +0.08087 dB FG PSNR,
-0.30977 dB full PSNR, -0.015890 mSSIM, +0.034741 mLPIPS, -0.000265 IoU,
-0.003253 FN and +0.003600 FP. The small FG PSNR edge and lower missing area do
not establish overall quality superiority. No repeated-seed uncertainty estimate
has been measured. Visual inspection finds over-smoothed Shell coverage and
coarse, high-contrast Strand strokes; similar FG PSNR does not imply equal texture.

Recorded host GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 (24 GiB).
Device-wide memory includes other resident allocations and is not process memory.
FPS is the existing native benchmark result, not a kernel-only or parameter-count
controlled benchmark. Ours includes primitive materialization; it is slower than
compact HairGS under the recorded execution paths.

HairGS compression took approximately 11 seconds from log timestamps; it was not
independently timed. Dense training + compression estimate + fine-tuning totals
4,166.82 seconds. This is **not a fully instrumented end-to-end total**: adapter
preparation, point-to-segment conversion, evaluation and benchmarking are excluded.

Ours FP32 state tensors occupy 9,051,752 bytes; its full checkpoint, including
optimizer/RNG/history, is 30,314,905 bytes. Its 8,262,104-byte static NPZ does not
have a validated runtime loader and is not advertised as a deployable package.
HairGS final native PLYs occupy 170,019,027 bytes unrestricted and 17,863,534 bytes
compact. Different formats/SH orders prevent a strict byte-for-byte efficiency claim.

Ours first/last complete 2K training-cycle mean losses are .02490735/.01935001.
HairGS final unrestricted/compact losses are .02619568/.03335693. Loss definitions
differ, so these values are convergence records, not a method ranking.

Ours worst test-view largest FN/FP component fractions are .00015336/.00733035;
these image-space components are not a 3D hole test. Fur-OTC is **not scored**:
there is no validated root/component/strand correspondence or physical-unit
calibration. The visual hull is an outer fur envelope, not recovered skin.

## Reproduction entry points and limitations

- Ours: `hairgs_hybrid/teacher_free_shell_strand_v1/run.py`,
  `run_formal8k_v1.json`, `hairgs_hybrid/export_teacher_free_shell_strand.py`.
- HairGS: `hairgs_hybrid/prepare_a050_hairgs_rgb_only.py`,
  `hairgs_hybrid/run_a050_hairgs_rgb_only_controls_v1.sh`, plus the checked-in
  native trainer, conversion, compression, renderer benchmark and external exporter.
- Source allowlist: `hairgs_hybrid/rgb_only_init_v1/source_tail_v3.json`.
  Only `core.py`, its package initializer and this source lock are required from
  the older RGB initialization folder; its unused COLMAP/MVS helpers are not
  called by the teacher-free path. An existing legacy topology module is imported
  by the compressor for its unchanged schema constant, not for teacher-free fitting.
- Runtime: WSL Ubuntu-22.04, Conda `unifur`, PyTorch 2.1.2+cu118, the pinned
  `third_party/hair-gs` submodule/native extensions and compatibility shim.
  Entry points/configuration currently contain owner-machine absolute paths.
  Data and compiled dependencies must be provisioned; a clone alone is not a
  portable, data-complete reproduction package.
- This is the frozen completed implementation. Checkpoint rendering reload passed
  a pixel-exact reference-view test, but strict interrupted optimizer/RNG resume
  is unsupported. Do not relaunch archived runners into partial output folders.
- No new training, model upload, data upload or output deletion accompanies this
  source release. Unrelated local experiments remain outside this selective commit.
